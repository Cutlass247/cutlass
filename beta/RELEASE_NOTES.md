# Cutlass 0.1.0 — Windows beta

The first public build of Cutlass: a video editor where your transcript is your timeline, grading is one click, and everything runs on your machine.

## Download

**Cutlass_0.1.0_x64-setup.exe** (Windows 10/11, 64-bit) — attached below.

On first run, Windows SmartScreen may warn *"Windows protected your PC"* because this beta isn't code-signed yet. Click **More info → Run anyway**. Then launch **Cutlass** from the Start menu.

## In this build

- **Edit by transcript** — on-device transcription; delete words to cut the video.
- **Smart cuts** — one-click removal of filler words and silences.
- **One-click grading** — Looks, `.cube` LUTs, color controls, sharpen/grain/vignette, green screen.
- **Auto-captions** from your transcript.
- **Titles, transitions, keyframes, speed/retime, multi-track.**
- **Export** — hardware-accelerated H.264, plus ProRes and WebM.
- **Projects** — save/open `.cutlass` files (double-click to open); optional auto-save.

## Known limitations

- **Windows only** for now. macOS/Linux later.
- **H.265 is hidden** — it depends on GPU HEVC support that not all machines have. H.264 works everywhere.
- **Export at your source resolution.** Upscaling (e.g. 1080p → 4K) lowers quality; the app defaults to your footage's resolution and warns if you go higher.
- **Not code-signed yet**, so expect the SmartScreen prompt above.
- Early beta — rough edges expected.
